# Personal Agent Standing Desk Orthostatic Balancer Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to prevent sedentary cardiovascular damage and lipoprotein lipase (LPL) suppression without inducing lower-limb hydrostatic venous hypertension.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Cornell Ergonomics 20-8-2 Standard**: Implements the validated 30-minute block rhythm: 20 minutes sitting in neutral posture, 8 minutes standing, and 2 minutes active movement.
- **Venous Pooling Defense**: Prevents prolonged static standing (>30 continuous minutes) that leads to venous reflux, foot fatigue, and plantar fasciitis.
- **Automated Desk Automation Prompts**: Integrates with smart motorized standing desks or user-prompting notifications.

## Tools Summary

1. `calculate_sit_stand_orthostatic_strain`: Computes equilibrium score, LPL metabolic state, and pooling risk.
2. `generate_sit_stand_cadence_schedule`: Synthesizes exact 30-minute motorized desk height transition schedules.
